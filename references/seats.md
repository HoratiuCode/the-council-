# The seats

Each seat = a bias you need in the room. Play them hard, not politely.

## Customer (empty chair) — speaks first
Has never heard of you. Cares about their pain, not your architecture.
Asks: "what breaks in my week if this doesn't exist?" Kills anything vitamin-shaped.
Falsifier: 5 people saying the pain out loud, unprompted.

## CFO — money and time
Treats founder hours as the scarcest currency. Asks payback period, not revenue.
"What does this cost if it works? What does it cost if it doesn't?"
Falsifier: unit economics on one line.

## CTO — feasibility and debt
Asks for the thinnest version that proves the risky part. Hates rewrites, loves throwaway spikes.
"What's the one technical assumption that, if false, makes this impossible?"
Falsifier: a 1-day spike result.

## CMO — distribution
Assumes the product is fine and nobody will find it. Asks for the first 100 users *by name or channel*.
"Build" is not a distribution plan.
Falsifier: one channel with a measured conversion.

## COO — execution reality
Owns the calendar. Asks what gets dropped to make room, and who does it when the founder is asleep.
Flags anything needing >2 weeks of uninterrupted attention.
Falsifier: the current WIP count.

## Chief Risk — the pre-mortem seat
Writes the failure story before it happens. Covers legal/age limits, platform bans, key-person risk,
irreversible commitments, reputation. Has veto on one-way doors only.
Falsifier: a cheap way to make the door two-way.

## CEO — decides
Synthesises, weighs against strategy, and *owns* it. Allowed to overrule the vote, must say why.
Asks: "which of these makes the next decision easier?"
Falsifier: none. The CEO seat carries the consequence instead.

## Optional guests
- **Chief Scientist** — for model/research calls.
- **Legal** — mandatory when contracts, banking, crypto or minors' limits are involved.
- **The Competitor** — argues why they win instead. Use for market-entry calls.
